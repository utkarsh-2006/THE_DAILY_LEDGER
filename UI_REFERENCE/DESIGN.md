## Brand & Style

The design system embodies the high-stakes tactical atmosphere of an elite financial broadsheet fused with ruthless municipal statecraft. It captures the world of high finance, backroom syndicates, and rapid-fire boardroom acquisitions across Velora City. The UI merges the tangible, ink-saturated tactility of a prestigious morning daily newspaper (*The Financial Times*, *The Economist*, *The Wall Street Journal*) with the sharp operational discipline of a complex strategy board game.

Every screen reads as living print: fresh editorial ink on heavyweight archival stock, stamped warrants, authoritative double-ruled margins, and crisp financial registers. The target audience seeks tactical depth, ruthless competitive strategy, and tactile editorial polish. The interface avoids modern generic "glossy corporate" glassmorphism; instead, it relies on structural rules, razor-sharp typographic scale, dense data hierarchy, paper grain texture, and cold pressroom ink.

## Colors

The palette simulates the tangible materiality of cold ink struck against warm, tactile paper stock. 

- **Primary (`#151515`) — Carbon Ink:** The bedrock tone for all headlines, rule borders, primary actions, and key typographic anchors.
- **Secondary (`#A7833A`) — Brass & Gilded Ochre:** Used for sovereign player ranks, prestige property tiers, golden ledger seals, and active focus markers.
- **Tertiary (`#9F3030`) — Editorial Crimson / Breaking Red:** Dedicated to hostile takeovers, margin calls, market liquidations, alert bulletins, and emergency ordinances.
- **Neutral (`#E9E3D3`) — Warm Paper Parchment:** The default page surface, supported by canvas variations:
  - Base Board & Ledger Ground: `#E9E3D3`
  - Elevated Dossier Card Surface: `#F4EFE6`
  - Recessed Table / Ticker Floor: `#DFD8C4`
- **Functional Accents:**
  - **Financial Bull Green (`#315C46` / `#234735`):** Positive dividend yields, capital gains, and solvent balance indicators.
  - **Maritime Slate (`#26384A` / `#1A2836`):** Harbor logistics, municipal bond tiers, and coastal ward ownership.
  - **Editorial Rule Stroke (`#C7C0AE`):** The structural line separator for double-ruled newspaper framing.

## Typography

The typographic hierarchy enforces a disciplined four-tier system:
1. **Masthead & Editorial Headlines (`Newsreader`):** Imparts historical weight, investigative authority, and broadsheet dignity. Used for the game masthead, district titles, turn dispatches, and breaking syndicate events.
2. **Category Banners & Operational Badges (`Oswald`):** Condensed, capitalized, and punchy. Drives the stock ticker, stamp badges (`ACQUIRED`, `FORECLOSED`), section headers, and market quadrant labels.
3. **Data & Accounting Ledger (`JetBrains Mono`):** Tabular numbers, asset IDs, share allocations, bond percentages, roll outputs, and net worth tallies. Guarantees clean numeric alignment across competitive balance sheets.
4. **Interface Control & Rule Prose (`Inter`):** Neutral, modern legibility for tooltips, card descriptions, modal dialogs, and player interaction controls.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout philosophy mirrors a multi-column broadsheet front page.
- **Desktop (12 Columns):** Arranged into traditional newspaper columns (1-col stock sidebar, 7-col interactive Velora City district board, 4-col dynamic dossier and financial ledger).
- **Tablet (8 Columns):** Collapses ledger and municipal deeds into tabbed drawer panels, preserving the central map grid with a fixed top market ticker.
- **Mobile (4 Columns):** Converts to a vertical edition format with swipeable district cards, a sticky consolidated bottom financial drawer, and a rolling top ticker tape.

Spacing uses rhythmic intervals aligned to typographic leading. Column dividers are rendered as physical newspaper hairline rules (`1px solid #C7C0AE`) rather than vacant air, keeping data density purposeful and cohesive.

## Elevation & Depth

This system avoids floating digital shadows and blurred neomorphism. Depth is strictly tactile and print-inspired:
- **Baseline Elevation (The Ground):** The broadsheet newsprint (`#E9E3D3`) flat surface.
- **Surface Elevation (Dossiers & Deeds):** Parchment layers (`#F4EFE6`) framed by crisp double rules (a `2px #151515` border separated from an inner `1px #151515` line by `2px` of blank space).
- **Embossed & Debossed Tactility:** 
  - Pressed Elements (Dice trays, ticker tracks, input fields): Inner ink shadows (`inset 0 1px 3px rgba(21, 21, 21, 0.15)`).
  - Raised Physical Pieces (Player tokens, deed dossiers, action seals): Hard offset press shadows (`2px 2px 0px #151515`), creating the feel of heavyweight board game punch-tokens.
- **Ink Stamp Badges:** Overlaid at slight rotations (`-2deg` to `3deg`) with faint ink-bleed visual noise.

## Shapes

The shape vocabulary is strictly cut-sheet and angular (`roundedness: 0`). 
- Borders are sheared at clean 90-degree right angles, evoking printed newsprint cuts, mechanical typesetting blocks, stock certificate coupons, and rigid ledger tables.
- Card corners, button containers, stamp frames, and modal sheets have no border radius.
- Notched or chamfered corners (`4px` 45-degree cuts) are selectively applied exclusively to municipal deed tags and currency coupons to evoke vintage tear-off vouchers.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Editorial Button:** Solid `#151515` fill, `#F4EFE6` text, framed with sharp corners. Hover: translates `-1px, -1px` with a hard `#A7833A` 2px offset drop shadow.
- **Secondary Ledger Button:** `#F4EFE6` parchment fill, `#151515` double-ruled 1px border, `label-ticker` all-caps typography. Hover: `#DFD8C4` fill.
- **Breaking Action / Seizure Button:** `#9F3030` fill, white typography, reserved strictly for hostile acquisitions, forfeitures, and municipal strikes.

### Stock Ticker Tape
- A continuous horizontal ribbon traversing screen boundaries. `#151515` background with `#E9E3D3` mono text. District indexes, company tickers (`VLR.EX`, `HRB.CO`, `AURL`), price movements in `#315C46` (Gain ▲) or `#9F3030` (Loss ▼).

### Stamp Badges
- Heavy framed rectangles (`border: 2px solid`) containing uppercase `label-stamp` text (`APPROVED`, `ACQUIRED`, `LIQUIDATED`, `UNDERWRITTEN`). Rendered with slight skew and ink saturation.

### Property Deeds & Dossiers
- Styled as folded broadsheet inserts: header bar colored by city ward (e.g., `#26384A` for Grand Harbor, `#A7833A` for Aurelia), followed by property title in `headline-sm`, dividend yield in tabular `data-mono-md`, and double-ruled footer sections detailing mortgage values.

### Input Fields & Selectors
- Flat `#DFD8C4` background with `1px solid #151515` border. Text entry in `data-mono-md`. Focus state switches the border to a double-line effect with a `#A7833A` tint.

### Dice Rolling Hub
- A recessed debossed parchment tray lined with a subtle felt grain texture and gold-leaf hash border (`#A7833A`). Dice present high-contrast bone-ivory faces with deep carbon pip insets.
