## 5. District Overview

### 1. Old Quarter
*   **Identity:** Historic heart, retail markets, and heritage architecture.
*   **Economic Role:** High early-game accessibility and stable retail cashflow.
*   **Primary Sector:** Heritage Commerce
*   **Visual Language:** Cobblestone, red brick, classic streetlamps, market stalls.

### 2. Exchange District
*   **Identity:** The financial nervous system of Velora.
*   **Economic Role:** High-value corporate real estate with heavy finance exposure.
*   **Primary Sector:** Finance & Enterprise
*   **Visual Language:** Glass skyscrapers, ticker tapes, polished marble, plazas.

### 3. Leisure & Hospitality (Riverfront)
*   **Identity:** Entertainment, tourism, and waterfront luxury.
*   **Economic Role:** Balanced growth and hospitality hubs subject to seasonal market shifts.
*   **Primary Sector:** Leisure & Hospitality
*   **Visual Language:** Boardwalks, marinas, neon signs, luxury facades.

### 4. Ironworks
*   **Identity:** The grimy, efficient industrial engine of the city.
*   **Economic Role:** Cheap Foundation/Core properties excellent for heavy development.
*   **Primary Sector:** Industry & Logistics
*   **Visual Language:** Smokestacks, steel girders, freight yards, concrete.

### 5. North Heights
*   **Identity:** Residential estates, civic institutions, and universities.
*   **Economic Role:** Safe, resilient mid-tier properties providing defensive stability.
*   **Primary Sector:** Residential & Civic
*   **Visual Language:** Terraced housing, green parks, stone libraries.

### 6. West Aerodrome
*   **Identity:** Aviation, tech logistics, and modern campuses.
*   **Economic Role:** High-tier, late-game expansion focused on modern enterprise.
*   **Primary Sector:** Technology & Aviation
*   **Visual Language:** Sweeping terminal curves, radar dishes, minimalist glass tech-parks.

## 7. Cross-Catalogue Balance Audit

**Properties by Tier:**
*   Foundation: 6
*   Core: 6
*   Growth: 6
*   Landmark: 6

**Properties by Primary Sector:**
*   Equally distributed: 4 properties primarily dedicated to each of the 6 sectors. Secondary exposures bleed naturally across districts to create portfolio blending.

**Development Compatibility:**
*   Every property can build at least one Cashflow and one Resilience family.
*   Thematic restrictions (e.g., no Production Lines at the Mayor's Estate) enforce strategic variation.

**District Pacing:**
*   **Ironworks** offers the cheapest entry point (total asking price $1440), facilitating early monopolies.
*   **Exchange District** and **West Aerodrome** are the most expensive late-game districts, requiring significant capital to control.
*   No district is mathematically strictly superior; lower-tier properties yield a higher ROI (18%) than Landmark properties (15%), ensuring value across the board.

## 8. Design Principles
1.  **Identity Over Spreadsheets:** Every property is a real place in Velora City, logically dictating its sector and development limits.
2.  **No Dead Space:** Foundation properties are cheap and high-yield; Landmarks are expensive but offer massive raw cashflow and powerful Flagship potential.
3.  **Natural Cross-Pollination:** Secondary sector exposures encourage trading and prevent players from being entirely immune or totally vulnerable to a single Market Shock.
